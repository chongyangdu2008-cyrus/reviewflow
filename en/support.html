ReviewFlow Support

Last updated: October 21, 2026

ReviewFlow is a study review app that runs on your device: write down text and images, get the next review scheduled along a forgetting curve, receive a reminder when it is due, and give feedback on how well you remembered. No account, no ads.

Contact Us

For questions, suggestions or bug reports, email chongyangdu2008@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as we can.

To help us find the problem faster, please include:

  • your device model and iOS or iPadOS version;
  • the ReviewFlow version (Settings › General › iPhone Storage › ReviewFlow);
  • what happened and the steps to reproduce it, with screenshots if helpful (please don’t send content you’d rather keep private).

Requirements

Requires iOS 17.0 or iPadOS 17.0 or later. Works on iPhone and iPad.

The app is available in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean and Spanish. Change it in the app under Settings › Language.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. ReviewFlow has no accounts, ads or subscriptions, and everything works on your device.

Where is my data stored? Does it sync?

Your data stays on your device. It is never uploaded to a server and is not synced through iCloud. To move to a new device or keep a copy, tap Export Backup in Settings and save the full backup (images, review history and settings included) to Files or iCloud Drive; then use Import Backup on the other device.

What happens when I restore a backup?

Restoring replaces all current data and settings with the backup. You first see what the backup contains and confirm, and the app automatically keeps a safety backup of your data from before the restore. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes. Deleting the app removes the content stored on the device, so export a backup first. Note that deleting a single item takes effect immediately and there is no trash; deleting a folder asks you to confirm.

How are reviews scheduled?

Choose a feedback style in Settings. Two options (Not Remembered / Remembered) advance through intervals of 1, 2, 4, 7, 15 and 30 days, then stay at 30 days. Three options (Forgot / Remembered / Fluent) and four options (Forgot / Difficult / Remembered / Fluent) calculate the next interval from your feedback and the last actual interval, up to 365 days; you can also turn off “Adapt intervals from review feedback”. Only tapping a feedback button counts as a review — opening or searching an item does not change its schedule. Your most recent feedback can be undone within 10 minutes.

Why don’t I get due reminders?

Turn on Due reminders in the app’s Settings and allow notifications in Settings › Notifications › ReviewFlow. A Focus may also silence them. Reminders are local notifications on your device and do not need the internet.

How do I use the Home Screen widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and search for ReviewFlow. The medium and large widgets let you tap a feedback button to complete a review (you may need to unlock your device first). The system decides when widgets refresh.

How do I add content from other apps?

In Safari, Photos, Notes and other apps, tap Share and choose Add to ReviewFlow to save text, a web link or images (up to 8 at a time). You can also use ReviewFlow’s Add Text and Add Images actions in the Shortcuts app.

Can I search the text in images?

Yes. Text in images is recognized on your device by Apple’s text recognition and is never uploaded. The first recognition can take a few seconds.

Are there limits on images?

Each item can hold up to 8 images, each up to 25 MB and 60 megapixels.
