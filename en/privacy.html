ReviewFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

ReviewFlow (“the app”) is developed and provided by an individual developer. We take your privacy seriously: the app does not collect, upload or sell any of your personal information. This policy explains how the app handles the data created when you use it on your device.

1. Information We Don’t Collect

  • The app has no account sign-up or sign-in.
  • The app shows no ads and includes no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or other third-party SDKs.
  • The app does not track you, does not read the advertising identifier, and does not link your data with data from other companies.
  • The developer runs no servers, and the app never sends your content to the developer or to any third party.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The content you enter or add in the app (folders, titles, text, images, tags and importance), together with your review history, review schedule and app settings, is stored only on your device — in storage shared by the app and its widget and share extension — and is protected by iOS data protection.

To let you search text inside images, the app recognizes that text on your device and keeps the results as a local cache. The developer cannot access any of this data.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you tap Take Photo, to photograph content you want to review.
  • Photos: you pick images with the system photo picker, and the app receives only the images you select.
  • Notifications: used to schedule due reminders on your device (local notifications). You can turn them off at any time.

You can change these permissions in the system Settings app at any time.

4. System Features from Apple

Text recognition in images uses Apple’s Vision framework on your device. The Home Screen widget, the share extension and the Shortcuts actions run on your device. When you use the app’s actions through Siri or the Shortcuts app, Apple processes the related data under its own privacy policy.

5. Backups and iCloud

The app does not use iCloud sync. You can export a backup manually and save it to a location you choose, such as the Files app or iCloud Drive. A backup contains all your content, images, review history and settings and is not encrypted, so keep it safe and don’t share it with anyone you don’t trust. If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, iOS may include the app’s data in your device backup, which Apple handles.

6. Purchases

If you buy the app on the App Store, Apple processes the payment. The developer does not receive your payment details or any information that identifies you.

7. When You Contact Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer and resolve your request, never for any other purpose, and we do not share it with third parties.

8. Children’s Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual items or folders in the app; deleting the app also deletes the data stored on the device (but not backup files you exported or device backups). Because the developer does not hold your data, we cannot recover or delete it for you.

10. Your Rights

Under applicable laws, such as China’s Personal Information Protection Law and the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal information. Since the app does not collect your personal information, you can view, change, export (Export Backup) and delete your data directly on your device. If you have any questions, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, email chongyangdu2008@gmail.com. See also Support.
